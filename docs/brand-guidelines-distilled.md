# Auckland Council Brand Guidelines — distilled reference

Source: *AC Brand Guidelines June 2025* (64pp, `attached_assets/AC_Brand_Guidelines_June_2025_*.pdf`),
*26-PRO-0516 Brand Guidelines contractors 2026*, and the live Frontify portal
(https://aucklandcouncil.frontify.com/d/67jM5b5GuoL9). The operational subset lives in
`brands.guidelines` (DB) and feeds every generation prompt; this file is the full reference.

## Brand idea

- **One purpose, one brand**: create an Auckland we can all be proud of. Every touchpoint uses the
  pōhutukawa and kotahitanga patterns so work is attributed back to the council.
- **Altogether Auckland** is the brand expression; kotahitanga (togetherness) is the core theme.
- Strapline: **"Tāmaki Turuki. Altogether Auckland."** — always lead with the te reo line.
- Pōhutukawa symbolism: blossom = people coming together (kotahitanga), leaves = guardianship of
  the environment (kaitiakitanga), waves = the waters that flank Tāmaki Makaurau. Chosen in 2010
  when the eight councils amalgamated.

## Voice & tone

- Inclusive: dial up personal pronouns — you, your, our, we, us.
- Talk like an Aucklander, not a bureaucrat: *let us know* (not "notify us"), *have your say*
  (not "consult with us"), *aim/goal* (not "objective").
- Headlines: no trailing full stop. Full stops on body copy only when there is other punctuation.
- Supporting copy under a headline: max three lines.
- Business-unit attribution goes in body copy where required: "Auckland Council Pools and
  Leisure.", "Auckland Council Holiday Parks.", "Auckland Council Holiday Places.",
  "Auckland Council Libraries."

## Pōhutukawa tile, grid, logos

- **Master logo** = colour pōhutukawa in a **white square tile**. The mark is vertically aligned to
  1/8 of the box (1/8 margin top and bottom), horizontally centred.
- The tile is the **building block of the grid**: divide the artwork's shortest axis by an even
  number of tiles — 4, 6 or 8 for print ("A" sizes usually 6), **1, 2 or 4 for digital**. One
  division = tile size. Page margin = **1/3 tile height**.
- Placement: primarily **bottom-right** of campaign artwork.
- Colour logo on white or ≤20% colour/image density backgrounds; single-colour (white) version on
  secondary colours, limited circumstances only.
- **Corporate logos** (traditional lockups with wordmark) are reserved for: third-party funding
  acknowledgement, building control, food safety, council/waste vehicles, all signage, uniforms,
  contractor branding, documents/stationery.
- Events: "Auckland Council Events" logo when council is majority funder; "Proudly supported by
  Auckland Council Events" when partially funded. Clear space unit u = pōhutukawa height / 5;
  minimum logo height 10mm / 50px. Full-colour versions on white/light backgrounds only.
- Auckland "A" destination logo: only when >50% of audience is outside Auckland; teal #00a6a7.

## The anther (framing device)

- A large circle on a straight stem: circle centred horizontally with clear space for headline
  copy above; stem enters from **left, lower-left 45°, or bottom centre — never 45° from the
  lower-right**, and never covered by the pōhutukawa tile.
- Size: extend to the margins, as big as reasonably possible, always showing straight stem lines.
  Cropped anthers are subject to design review.
- Stroke options: solid (same thickness as the logo's wave), gradient (double thickness), or none.
- As a housing device: either copy inside the anther (image outside) or image inside (copy
  outside). Backgrounds behind copy get a colour/opacity/multiply effect and must pass
  accessibility contrast. Full-bleed photography variants: blur outside the anther (image inside in
  focus) or the anther as a simple intersecting outline.
- Not used inside carousel frames (makes imagery too small).

## Kotahitanga patterns

- Built from pōhutukawa elements + three tohu: **Te Ao Tūroa** (the enduring world),
  **Whanaungatanga** (strengthening relationships), **Taonga Tuku Iho** (treasures handed on).
- Used as subtle background texture: three-row wallpaper 30/20/10% opacity (bottom→top), two-row
  20/10%, single row 20–30%. **Never more than 30% opacity.** Behind mid-artwork images ≤30%.
- Stamp treatment: 2–3 patterns snapped to the grid, usually next to the pōhutukawa tile.
- Patterns may use any brand colour.

## Typography

- **National 2** family (Kris Sowersby, Klim Type Foundry).
  - Headlines: **National 2 Condensed Bold, ALL CAPS**.
  - Headline support / subheads / CTAs / URLs: **National 2 Bold**.
  - Body copy: **National 2 Regular**.
  - **Rocked** script accent: sparingly, events/invite/celebrate collateral only.
- Strapline treatment: usually two lines ("Tāmaki Turuki." / "Altogether Auckland."), height = 1/3
  of the tile, aligned to margins; can run single-line or right-aligned beside the tile. Leading =
  point size +15%. Te reo always first.
- **Search-bar CTA**: pill with "Search" in National 2 Regular (tracking −10) + phrase in National
  2 Bold (tracking +10), e.g. "Search dog registration". Can also carry a short URL.
- **Hotspots**: short ALL-CAPS message set in a kotahitanga circle (e.g. "AK HAVE YOUR SAY");
  crafted National 2 Condensed Bold product/programme marks (PEST FREE AUCKLAND, LIVE LIGHTLY) are
  type treatments, **not logos**.

## Illustration style

- Simple **flat vector**, snaps to the grid; built from logo elements — circles/ellipses from the
  anther, leaf shapes, water curves. Rounded corners; arms as single heavy strokes with round caps.
- **Three-plane scenes**: background, mid-ground (subject), foreground; a repeated object must be
  ≥30% bigger in the foreground.
- Established cast: faceless diverse people; native birds (tūī, pīwakawaka, kererū, korimako,
  tauhou, riroriro, pūkeko, takahē); animals incl. dogs, ducks, hens, cows, sheep, horses, pigs.

## Photography & video

- Documentary style, the real Auckland: togetherness through diversity, pride, community; prefer
  more than one person in shot; rich/bright/vibrant colour; playgrounds, beaches, paths, community
  centres; avoid CBD-centric imagery. Landscapes and drone/aerials welcome. Events: vibrant,
  upbeat, optimistic.
- Video: animated pōhutukawa end-frame with strapline; watermark top-left; name keys are .mogrt
  Premiere templates; captions required; keep top-left free of hotspots and bottom-right clear
  (YouTube subscribe).

## Social media rules

- The pōhutukawa is the profile picture on council channels, so **1080×1080 organic/paid social
  tiles carry no logo**; Facebook cover 820×360: no logo, no patterns.
- Strapline is **not required** on social statics — "less is more"; on video it appears in the
  blurred outro end-frame (4:5 Meta video: whiteout mono pōhutukawa + strapline PNG on final frame).
- Meta content: first sentence carries the key message; keep creative single-minded (split multiple
  messages into versions); ALL-CAPS/exclamation marks in primary copy hurt quality scores;
  carousels 3–4 frames max, no anther in frames; CTA buttons/URLs unnecessary (links are overlaid).
- Reels = awareness/entertainment (15–30s in-feed); Stories = drive a specific action (≤10–15s or
  add a tap prompt).

## Standard formats seen in guidelines

Print/posters (A sizes), digital screens 1920×1080 & 1080×1920, digital ads 300×250, 300×600,
160×600, 728×90, video end-frames, social 1080×1350, 1200×628, 1080×1080 (no logo), Pinterest
1000×1500, Meta story 1440×2560 (safe zones), Facebook cover 820×360.

## Contractors (2026)

- Co-branded clothing on all council jobs; simplified AC logo (no Māori strapline / "working
  together with" text). Equal prominence with contractor logo. Placement order: wearer's left
  chest (min 28mm) → right chest → under contractor logo → top of left sleeve. Front of safety
  wear only, on solid colour. Full colour on white/light, white on dark, black on low-contrast/
  hi-vis. Direct embroidery.
